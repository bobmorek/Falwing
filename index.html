Not a safety service. Scores are a guide from forecast models. Offshore wind at the beaches blows you out into Falmouth Bay; if in doubt, don't go. Emergency on the water: call 999 or VHF 16 and ask for the Coastguard.

Where the data comes from. Wind, gusts and daylight from the UK Met Office model (UKV, about 2 km grid), through Open-Meteo, used under CC BY 4.0. Swell and modelled tide from Open-Meteo Marine, under the same licence. Measured waves from the Porthleven buoy: real-time data from the Regional Coastal Monitoring Programme (coastalmonitoring.org), made freely available under the Open Government Licence; these are real-time data and are not quality-controlled. Measured wind from the RNAS Culdrose weather report (METAR), through aviationweather.gov. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors under the ODbL, with map tiles by CARTO and seamarks from OpenSeaMap (CC BY-SA 2.0).

The spot scores, notes and code are FAL Wing's own. Spot settings are set by hand and may be wrong. The tide is modelled, not an official prediction: not for navigation, and never for timing a crossing.
